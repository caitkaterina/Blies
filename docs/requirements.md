Bases: South Africa, Gabon, UAE. Additional operating markets: Zimbabwe, Mozambique, Oman. Cross-border trucking reaches DRC. No other offices or asset ownership are implied.

Positioning: **The supply chain, handled.** Procurement and logistics are equal pillars. Source → Procure → Inspect → Document → Clear → Move → Deliver. Road fleet is owned. Sea/air are coordinated through networks and partners: no owned ships or aircraft.

Never fabricate customer identities, testimonials, logos, quantities, tonnages, offices, warehouses, port ownership, certifications, awards, relationships, financial/performance claims or vehicle specifications. Air is subject to cargo, route, carrier and regulation.

## Approval boundary

The app implements the development workflow. Professional translations, approved legal policies, actual brand/fleet assets, reconciled categories, final contacts, production connections, independent security testing and deployment validation are externally dependent launch requirements. Do not call this application publicly launch-ready while these remain outstanding. Preserve this distinction in further development and onboarding.
